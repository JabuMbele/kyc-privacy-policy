Privacy Policy for Know Your City (KYC)

Last Updated: October 9, 2026

Skies-Are-Blue Mobile Technologies operates the Know Your City App (KYC) mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: When you sign up or log in using social authentication (Google, Facebook, or X/Twitter), we collect basic profile information made available by those providers, such as your display name, email address, and profile picture.
  • User Feedback & Submissions: Information you voluntarily provide when submitting feedback, reporting bugs, or contacting support.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Location Data: With your explicit permission, the App collects precise or approximate location data from your device to show nearby landmarks, historical sites, and local points of interest.
  • Usage & Device Data: Diagnostic data, including device model, operating system version, app usage analytics, and performance metrics.
  • Audio & Media State: We monitor audio focus and playback states locally on your device to seamlessly pause or resume background audio. We do not record ambient audio.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate identity, personalize experience, and present location-relevant insights.
  • To monitor usage trends, fix bugs, resolve technical issues, and improve app functionality.

3. Third-Party Services & Authentication

  • Firebase Services (Google): For authentication and database management (Firebase Privacy Policy).
  • Google Sign-In: Google Privacy Policy
  • Facebook Login (Meta): Meta Privacy Policy
  • X Login: X Privacy Policy
